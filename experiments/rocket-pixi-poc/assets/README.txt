Drop your transparent rocket PNG here as:

  rocket.png

Recommended: tall portrait orientation, transparent background, nose-up,
reasonably high resolution (the sprite is displayed at ~66% of screen
height). If missing, main.js falls back to a generated placeholder and
shows an on-screen warning.

Nozzle attachment points are estimated as fractions of the image's own
width/height in main.js (search for NOZZLE_POINTS) — adjust those three
{x, y} fractions to match where your art's main/left/right nozzles
actually sit once you drop in the real asset.
