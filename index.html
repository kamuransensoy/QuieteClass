How QuietClass Works

  1. 1. Choose a Voice Level Tell QuietClass how much talking is allowed.
  2. 2. Set the Mission Time Choose how long your class will play.
  3. 3. Start the Mission QuietClass listens to the overall classroom noise level.
  4. 4. Keep the Mission Safe If the room gets too loud, the mission's energy meter rises.
  5. 5. Complete the Mission Keep the noise under control until the timer reaches zero.

Privacy

QuietClass measures overall sound level. It does not need to understand or record what students are saying.
